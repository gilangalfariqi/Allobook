About this book

A profound exploration of minimalism in modern architectural design. Rostova examines how the absence of ornamentation and the strategic use of negative space create environments that foster contemplation and focus. This comprehensive volume features over 200 high-quality photographs from seminal projects around the globe, making it an essential reference for students and practitioners alike.
